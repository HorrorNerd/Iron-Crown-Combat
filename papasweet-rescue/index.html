Story

MommaSweet defeated the villain inside a video game. He couldn't beat her there, so he reached into the real world for revenge. Now PapaSweet is roaming the city, making money, gathering gear, visiting churches for strength, and chasing clues to the lair of Dr. Glitchgrave.

  • N start a new run
