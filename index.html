🔱 Iron Crown Combat 🔱

Presented by SWA (Syndicate Wrestling Alliance)

Iron Crown Combat is a brutal, hybrid-rules fight league where only the strong endure.
Hosted in a wrestling ring, each fight is contested under 3 rounds of 5 minutes.
No judges—victory can only be earned by:

  • ✦ KO
  • ✦ Submission
  • ✦ Referee Stoppage
  • ✦ Time-limit Draw

All fighting styles are welcome—from pro wrestling and jiu-jitsu to kickboxing, sambo, and beyond.
Competitors come from all over the world to battle for supremacy, pride, and a piece of the purse.
